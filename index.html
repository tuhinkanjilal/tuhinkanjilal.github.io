Media & Broadcast Enterprise

Disaster Recovery Architecture — Design & Build

Designed and built the DR architecture for a critical broadcast infrastructure estate: on-premises stretched vSphere clusters replicating into a cloud-hosted stretched vSphere/vSAN SDDC, with a dedicated cloud DR region as the failover target.

  • 50 ESXi hosts across 5 clusters spanning 2 physical sites, with synchronous storage replication between on-prem availability zones
  • RTO reduced from 4 hours to ~2 hours through VMware Cloud DR and automated failover orchestration
  • Cloud DR region built on delta-based replication with a 30 min – 4 hr RPO, plus live-mount capability for non-disruptive DR testing and validation
  • Dual Direct Connect links with a transit gateway design for on-prem-to-cloud and inter-region connectivity
  • Traded on-prem DR infrastructure cost for simpler cloud-native failover orchestration; automated monthly failover tests turned DR validation into a routine, board-visible control
